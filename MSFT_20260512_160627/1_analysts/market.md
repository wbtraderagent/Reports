**Moving Average Summary:** Price is sandwiched between the 50 SMA (support below) and 200 SMA (resistance above), with the 10 EMA acting as immediate overhead resistance. This configuration is typical of a consolidation or recovery phase within a larger downtrend.

**MACD Summary:** While the MACD remains positive (bullish territory), the declining line and negative histogram suggest that upward momentum is deteriorating. This divergence between price (which has been relatively stable) and MACD (which is declining) could signal an impending pullback.

### 3. RSI Analysis

The RSI's neutral reading suggests that the market is in a consolidation phase, with neither buyers nor sellers in control. This is consistent with the price action, which has been range-bound between $405 and $433 over the past two weeks.

**RSI Summary:** Neutral RSI provides no directional bias. Traders should wait for the RSI to move toward overbought (>70) or oversold (<30) levels for clearer signals.

**ATR Summary:** Moderate volatility supports the use of standard position sizing. The 50 SMA at $398.55 is approximately 1.3x ATR below the current price, making it a logical stop-loss level for long positions.

### 5. Volume-Weighted Analysis (VWMA)

**VWMA Summary:** Price below VWMA indicates weakening volume-supported momentum. This corroborates the bearish signals from the MACD histogram and the price being below the 10 EMA.
