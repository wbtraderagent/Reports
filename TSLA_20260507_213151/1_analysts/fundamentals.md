## **Recommendation**  
**FINAL TRANSACTION PROPOSAL: HOLD**  
- **Rationale:** Tesla’s strong cash flow and revenue growth justify its high valuation, but the high debt-to-equity ratio and volatile beta pose risks. Investors should monitor:  
  1. Debt reduction progress (target: Debt/Equity < 5).  
  2. EV market share gains vs. competitors (e.g., Ford, Rivian).  
  3. Interest rate trends (rising rates could pressure debt costs).  

**Risk Alert:** Consider selling if debt metrics worsen or if the stock breaks below $350 (support level).
