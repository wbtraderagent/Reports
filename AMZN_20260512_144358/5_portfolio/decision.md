## Executive Summary

After synthesizing the debate between our Aggressive, Conservative, and Neutral Risk Analysts, I am authorizing a **Buy** recommendation for `AMZN`. While the Conservative Analyst correctly identifies significant technical exhaustion (14% extension above the 50-day SMA) and fundamental leverage risks (Total Debt rising to $209B), the Aggressive Analyst’s thesis on structural dominance in AI and logistics remains the primary alpha driver. 

However, we will not execute with the full exposure or wide stop-loss parameters proposed by the Aggressive Analyst. Instead, I am adopting the **Neutral Analyst's** risk-adjusted execution strategy to mitigate the volatility concerns raised by the Conservative side while capturing the upside potential identified by the Bull case in our Research Report.

## Synthesis of Risk Debate

### 1. Fundamentals: Strategic Investment vs. Solvency Stress
*   **The Conflict:** The Aggressive Analyst argues that the negative Q1 Free Cash Flow (-$18B) is a strategic deployment of $44B in CapEx for AI infrastructure ("Amazon Now"), supported by robust Operating Cash Flow ($26B). The Conservative Analyst counters that this represents solvency stress, citing Total Debt increasing from $153B to $209B in one quarter.
*   **PM Verdict:** We accept the Aggressive view on capital allocation but respect the Conservative warning on leverage. The negative FCF is indeed a feature of growth (building moats), not operational bleeding. However, the debt load requires strict position sizing limits to prevent portfolio fragility if execution delays occur in Q2/Q3.

### 2. Technicals: Momentum vs. Exhaustion
*   **The Conflict:** The Aggressive Analyst dismisses MACD divergence and RSI cooling as noise, pointing to institutional accumulation above the VWMA ($265.87). The Conservative Analyst views the 14% extension above the 50-day SMA ($232) as a signal for inevitable mean reversion.
*   **PM Verdict:** We acknowledge the Neutral Analyst's assessment that waiting for a pullback to $230 risks missing the breakout, but entering fully at current levels ignores the exhaustion signals. The VWMA support is valid, but we must respect the volatility indicated by an ATR of $6.86.

### 3. Risk Management: Drawdown Tolerance
*   **The Conflict:** The Aggressive Analyst proposes a stop loss at $230 (below the 50-day SMA), accepting a potential 14% drawdown. The Conservative Analyst argues this violates capital preservation principles given the concentration risk (`AMZN`, Alphabet, Meta driving 70% of S&P growth).
*   **PM Verdict:** A 14% drawdown on a single name is excessive. We will adopt the Neutral Analyst's recommendation to tighten the stop loss to $245 (near the Lower Bollinger Band at $241.85) to protect against trend invalidation without being shaken out by normal volatility swings.
