### B. Competitive Positioning & Technology

1. **Nvidia Challenger**: Cerebras claims its chips can perform AI inference work faster than Nvidia's GPUs, which are less specialized for inference
2. **Unique Architecture**: Cerebras chips take up the entire surface of a silicon wafer, unlike traditional microchips that etch dozens of chips from one wafer
3. **OpenAI Partnership**: Cerebras has joined OpenAI's "inner circle," suggesting strong enterprise demand and strategic validation
4. **Digi Power X Deal**: Signed a 10-year, $1.1 billion exclusive AI data center colocation agreement, demonstrating long-term infrastructure commitments
