## 2. Company Fundamentals & Competitive Positioning

### Business Model
Cerebras manufactures **wafer-scale AI chips**—chips the size of a dinner plate that utilize the entire silicon wafer rather than etching dozens of microchips from it. This unique architecture allows for:
- **Faster inference work** compared to Nvidia's GPUs
- **Specialized optimization** for AI inference (vs. general-purpose GPU workloads)
- **Higher compute density** per chip

### Strategic Partnerships
- **OpenAI Partnership**: Cerebras has "joined OpenAI's inner circle," suggesting deep integration with one of the world's leading AI companies. This is a significant validation of Cerebras' technology and a competitive moat against Nvidia.
- **Digi Power X (NEOE:DGX)**: Signed a 10-year, $1.1 billion exclusive AI data center colocation agreement with Cerebras, demonstrating long-term infrastructure commitments.

### Competitive Landscape
| Competitor | Cerebras Advantage |
|------------|-------------------|
| **Nvidia** | Faster inference performance; wafer-scale architecture; specialized for AI workloads |
| **AMD** | Similar wafer-scale approach but with less market traction |
| **Custom AI Chips (Google TPU, etc.)** | More accessible to broader AI developers |
