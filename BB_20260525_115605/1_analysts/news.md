- The stock's rapid ascent has outpaced its **1-year total return and longer-term performance**, suggesting potential overextension.

### 💡 Trading Implications for BB
- **Bull case**: FedRAMP certification locks in federal contracts; QNX's dominance in automotive/embedded OS provides a durable moat; physical AI/robotics demand is accelerating.
- **Bear case**: The stock has run up over 100% in a year; profit-taking and mean-reversion risks are elevated; valuation may not yet be supported by earnings.
